Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the RideForge iPhone game ("the game") collects, how it's used and the choices you have. The game is made by One Life Studios LLC ("we", "us").

Short version: you can play without an account, and we never ask for your email, contacts, photos or location. The game uses Unity Gaming Services for online features, Google AdMob for ads, Apple iCloud to sync your saves (if you turn it on), and Apple for purchases.

Information stored on your phone

Your rides, park, coins, unlocks and settings are saved on your device. We don't receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on Save to iCloud in Settings, your rides and progress are copied to your own iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple ID; we can't see it.

Online features

Publishing rides, the community browser, likes, follows and Ride Together / Ride Roulette use Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies. The first time you use an online feature, the game signs you in anonymously and Unity assigns a random player ID. Depending on what you do, we store:

  • your creator name (the name you pick in the game)
  • rides you choose to publish: the ride's design, title, world and date
  • likes and play counts on published rides, the creators you follow and your follower count
  • reports you send about other players' rides
  • while you're in a Ride Together or Ride Roulette room: your creator name, emotes and ride scores

Published rides, your creator name, like and play counts and follower counts are visible to other players. In Ride Together and Ride Roulette, other riders in the room see your creator name, emotes and scores. There is no text or voice chat. Please don't use your real name or personal information as your creator name or in ride titles.

Creator names and ride titles pass through a word filter. You can report any ride and block any creator, and we review reports and remove content and creators that break the rules.

When you use online features, your device's IP address is processed by Unity's servers to provide the service.

Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Sign in with Apple (optional)

You can link your player ID to your Apple ID with Sign in with Apple so your published rides, likes and follows carry over to a new device. Apple gives us a random identifier for your account. We don't request your name or email address.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • device and app information (model, iOS version, language, app version)
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • how you interact with ads
  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks

Google uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. If you don't allow tracking, you'll still see ads, just less personalized ones. If you're in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

In-app purchases (like Remove Ads) are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock it.

We don't sell your personal information. We don't collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and the game doesn't send gameplay analytics to us.

Your choices

  • Tracking: turn it off anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ads: the Remove Ads purchase removes all forced ads. Optional reward videos stay available because you choose to watch them.
  • Published rides: unpublish any ride anytime from My Rides.
  • Delete your online data: email us (address below) and we'll delete your creator name, published rides, likes and follows.
  • Delete local data: delete the app (and turn off iCloud sync first if you want iCloud copies removed).

How long we keep data

Published rides stay until you unpublish them or ask us to delete them. Room data is deleted when the room closes. Reports are kept for as long as needed to review them. Unity and Google keep data according to their own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or the UK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to or limit how it's used. Email us to make a request. We'll respond within the time the law requires.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information (for example, a real name as a creator name), contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
