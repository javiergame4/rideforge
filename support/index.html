Common questions

How do I make a coaster?

Tap New Ride and draw a line with your finger. RideForge builds the track, supports and banking for you. Add loops, corkscrews and more from the side panel, then tap Ride.

How do I get a huge first drop?

Tap Start in the editor and drag the height slider (or pick a preset) to raise your station on a tower up to 110 m. You can also drag the station to move it and flip which way the train leaves.

The editor says my ride needs fixing

Tap the warning to see what's wrong: usually a turn that's too tight or a special part that doesn't have enough room. Redraw that bit with a smoother line, or move the part.

How do I share a ride with a friend?

In My Rides, tap the share button to send a ride code. Your friend copies it and taps Paste in My Rides to ride the exact same track.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads in the Shop. It removes all forced ads forever. Optional reward videos (like free coins) stay available because you choose to watch them.

I bought Remove Ads but I'm still seeing ads

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. If that doesn't fix it, email us.

I got a new phone. How do I get my rides back?

Turn on Save to iCloud in Settings on your old phone, then on the new phone with the same Apple ID. Purchases come back with Restore Purchases.

Someone published something inappropriate

Tap the flag on the ride to report it. You can also block the creator so you never see their rides. We review reports and remove content that breaks the rules.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Unpublish your rides from My Rides, or email us and we'll delete all your online data. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
